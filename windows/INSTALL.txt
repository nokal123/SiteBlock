SiteBlock for Windows - installing on this PC

SiteBlock blocks the websites you choose in every browser and app on this PC, for everyone who uses it.
It also always blocks adult websites: it sets this PC's DNS servers to the CleanBrowsing Family Filter (which
also blocks VPN and proxy sites and mixed-content sites such as Reddit, and forces SafeSearch and YouTube's
restricted mode) and turns on the adult-site filter and SafeSearch in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox. Only
uninstalling SiteBlock removes this.
Unblocking a site needs the SiteBlock password and then a waiting period, and uninstalling needs the password
and then a 48-hour wait. Only install it if the person who uses this PC wants it.

Requirements: Windows 11, or Windows 10 version 2004 or newer, on an Intel, AMD or Arm PC, and an
administrator account (or someone who knows an administrator password).


INSTALL

1. Unzip SiteBlock-Windows.zip: right-click it, choose "Extract All...", then click Extract.
   Keep the SiteBlock folder together; the installer needs everything in it.

2. Open the SiteBlock folder, right-click "Install SiteBlock.cmd" and choose "Run as administrator".
   (Double-clicking it also works: it then asks for administrator rights itself.)

3. Windows may show a blue box that says "Windows protected your PC". This is Windows SmartScreen. It warns
   about programs that few people have downloaded and that are not signed with a paid certificate, which is
   true of SiteBlock. Click "More info", then click "Run anyway".

4. Windows asks "Do you want to allow this app to make changes to your device?". Click Yes. On an account
   that is not an administrator, type an administrator's password here.

5. A black window shows the installer working. It copies SiteBlock into C:\Program Files\SiteBlock, sets up
   two background services that keep the block in place, makes SiteBlock start every time someone signs in,
   and then checks that everything works. Lines that start with SKIP are normal before you block a site.
   When it says "SiteBlock is installed", press any key to close the window.


THE SHIELD ICON

SiteBlock lives in a shield icon by the clock, at the right end of the taskbar.
Click it, type a site (for example youtube.com) and press Block. The first time, SiteBlock asks you to choose
your SiteBlock password. Keep that password safe: it cannot be shown again.

Windows often hides new icons. If you cannot see the shield, click the small ^ arrow by the clock: the shield
is in the box that opens. To keep it always visible, drag the shield from that box onto the taskbar next to the
clock. (On Windows 11 you can also open Settings > Personalization > Taskbar > Other system tray icons and
turn SiteBlock on.)

Chrome and Edge: while any site is blocked, their menus say "Managed by your organization". That only refers to
SiteBlock's block list.


UPDATES

SiteBlock checks for a newer version once a day. When there is one, the panel shows a banner with an Update
button. Click Update, then click Yes when Windows asks for permission. Your blocked sites, your password and
every countdown are kept.


UNINSTALL

Click the shield icon, open Settings, and choose Uninstall. It asks for your SiteBlock password, and then
SiteBlock removes itself after 48 hours (or after your unblock delay, if that is longer).
The same works from a terminal with: siteblock uninstall


FORGOTTEN PASSWORD

Open the Start menu, type "Terminal" (or "PowerShell"), right-click it and choose "Run as administrator".
Then type this and press Enter:

    siteblock recover

The password is cleared after 48 hours, and you choose a new one the next time you block a site.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

Running "Install SiteBlock.cmd" again repairs SiteBlock and keeps your blocked sites and password.
SiteBlock's logs are in C:\ProgramData\SiteBlock\Logs. They never contain your sites or your password.
